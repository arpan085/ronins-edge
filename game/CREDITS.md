# Credits

Ronin's Edge is built in Godot 4.3. The world, props, most textures and the enemy
characters are generated by the project's own scripts (`v2/`, `v3/`, `v4/`, `tools/` in the
dev-tools zip). Everything else comes from the free sources below. **Every third-party
asset is CC0 1.0 (public domain) except the fonts, which are SIL Open Font
Licence 1.1** (texts in `assets/fonts/LICENSE.txt`), **and the wolf model, which is
CC-BY 4.0** (CoinCoin — see *Animals*). CC0 needs no attribution; it is given here
with thanks.

## Ground textures (v0.13)

From **Poly Haven** (polyhaven.com, CC0 1.0), packed by `tools/make_ground_arrays.py`:
Sparse Grass, Forest Leaves 02, Rocky Trail, Mossy Rock, Brown Mud 02, River Small Rocks,
Aerial Grass Rock (macro variation). The water noise, foam and waterfall textures are
generated (`tools/make_water_noise.py`).

## Tree bark and rock (v0.14)

From **Poly Haven** (polyhaven.com, CC0 1.0), in `assets/textures/bark/` and `assets/textures/rock/`:
Sakura Bark, Pine Bark, Japanese Cedar Bark, Trident Maple Bark (tree trunks and limbs), and
Mossy Rock again for rocks and loose stones. Leaf and petal textures are generated
(`tools/make_foliage_atlas.py`).
